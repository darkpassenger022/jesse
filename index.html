Methodology

Four steps behind every research brief.

A disciplined workflow helps separate observable market information from assumptions.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the change

Identify unusual movement, liquidity changes, volume shifts or on-chain activity that deserves attention.

02 / VERIFY

Check the context

Cross-check multiple data points and look for obvious contradictions before drawing conclusions.

03 / MONITOR

Follow the signal

Watch whether the underlying activity persists, changes or disappears as the market develops.

04 / BRIEF

Communicate clearly

Present the observation, context and risks without promising an outcome that cannot be known in advance.
